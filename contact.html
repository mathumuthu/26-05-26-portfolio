Frequently
asked

Quick answers to common questions.

[01]What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A typical website takes 4-8 weeks, while a full-stack application may take 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines during our discovery phase and keep you updated throughout.

[02]How do you handle project communication?

We use Slack for daily communication, hold weekly video standups, and provide access to our project management tool. You'll have a dedicated project manager as your single point of contact throughout the engagement.

[03]Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes! We offer flexible maintenance and support packages. This includes bug fixes, security updates, performance monitoring, and feature enhancements. Most clients opt for our monthly retainer for peace of mind.

[04]What is your pricing model?

We offer both fixed-price projects and time-and-materials engagements. Fixed-price works best for well-defined scopes, while T&M is ideal for agile projects with evolving requirements. We'll recommend the best fit during our initial consultation.

[05]Can you work with our existing team?

Absolutely. We regularly collaborate with in-house teams, acting as an extension of your workforce. We can integrate with your existing workflows, tools, and processes to ensure seamless collaboration.

[06]Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, we routinely sign NDAs and confidentiality agreements. We understand the sensitive nature of many projects and take data security and intellectual property protection very seriously.
