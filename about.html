Our

EST.
2021

From a small garage to a global digital agency

Stackly was founded in 2021 by a group of college friends who shared a common vision: to create digital products that truly make a difference. What started as a small freelance operation has grown into a full-service software development company with clients across 15 countries.

We believe that great software is born at the intersection of cutting-edge technology and human-centered design. Every project we undertake is driven by a deep understanding of user needs and business goals.

Today, our team of 25+ professionals works remotely from around the world, bringing diverse perspectives and expertise to every challenge. We have delivered over 70 successful projects ranging from fintech platforms to AI-powered applications.
